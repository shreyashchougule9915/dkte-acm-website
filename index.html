Who We Are

Empowering Tech Leaders

The ACM Student Chapter DKTE is dedicated to promoting knowledge and advancing the profession of computer science. Through interactive workshops, hackathons, and seminars, we bridge the gap between academia and industry.

Our mission is to foster a culture of continuous learning and collaboration, providing members with the resources and network they need to succeed in the ever-evolving tech landscape.
